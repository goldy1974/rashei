# Rashei · ראשי תיבות
Photograph or paste Hebrew Torah text; abbreviations (ר״ת) are expanded and highlighted in gold with full Hebrew + English, other words get an English gloss.

## Put it on your iPhone Home Screen
1. Open the app link in **Safari**.
2. Tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen** → **Add**.
3. Open it once while online and take one test photo — that saves the Hebrew reader so it works offline afterwards.

## Using it
- **📷 Take photo** opens the iPhone camera; **🖼️ Choose photo** picks from Photos. Text is read on the phone (no upload) and placed in the box — fix any misread letters, then tap **Expand & translate**.
- Or paste/type Hebrew and tap **Expand & translate**. **Demo text** shows an example.
- Gold = known abbreviation (tap/hover for full form). Red = looks like an abbreviation but isn't in the dictionary. Grey italics = word not in the glossary.
- Search the dictionary at the bottom in Hebrew or English.

## Files
`index.html` app · `dict.js` abbreviations (`abbr|full Hebrew|English`) · `gloss.js` word glossary · `vendor/` Tesseract.js 5 + Hebrew model (offline OCR) · `sw.js` offline cache.
